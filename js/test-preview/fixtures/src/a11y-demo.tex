\textbf{Part (a)}

Find the matrix $A$ of $T$, given the values below.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{cc}
$x$ & $T(x)$ \\
$e_1$ & $(2,1)$ \\
$e_2$ & $(-1,1)$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Part (b)}

Compute $\det A$ and explain what it says about the area of the image of the unit square:
\[
  \det\begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix} = 2\cdot 1 - (-1)\cdot 1 .
\]

\textcolor{lightgray}{Hint: compare the area of the parallelogram with the area of the square.}
