GENERATED by `build/gen-presentation-showcase.mjs`; do not edit tables by
hand. Speech columns are REAL output (MathJax 4.1.3 + SRE, ClearSpeak
default) — nothing is mocked. "Rendered" holds the plain notation between $

Provenance notes: the \dot{x} equation is verbatim from APMTH 108's Spring
2025 stability notes (Strogatz course); x^* appears with DIFFERENT meanings
in APMTH 108 (fixed point) and APMTH 121 (optimal solution) — the two rows
share one notation on purpose. "concentration" is a real concept from the
W3C intent-editor open list (chemistry area); time-derivative, fixed-point,
optimal-solution, and gradient are not yet in any vocabulary — those rows
run on the extension's graceful fallback (hyphens become spaces), showing
the mechanism works ahead of vocabulary coverage.
